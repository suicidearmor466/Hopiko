## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**HoPiKo**, developed by Merge Games, is a fast-paced action platformer designed for Windows. In this thrilling game, players must save their enslaved HoPiKo brothers from a world overrun by a destructive virus known as the Nanobyte. The **latest version** of HoPiKo features hundreds of hand-crafted levels filled with intense challenges that will test your reflexes and skills to the limit. 

With its minimal control scheme and brutal gameplay, HoPiKo delivers a unique experience that pushes players to jump, avoid obstacles, and explore shortcuts, all while competing for the fastest times and unlocking special bonus runs. Get ready to immerse yourself in the **full functionality** of this electrifying game and enjoy an authentic chiptune soundtrack that enhances the overall adventure.
